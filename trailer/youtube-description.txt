Starship Flight Simulator | Launch, Catch the Booster, Reentry | Play Free in Your Browser

DESCRIPTION
Fly SpaceX's Starship and Super Heavy from the pad to orbit and back, free in your browser.

▶ Play now (on a computer): https://starship.wjacobs.space

Launch from Starbase, hand-fly the ascent through max-Q or let the autopilot fly, hot stage,
catch Super Heavy with the chopsticks, reach orbit, survive reentry and bring the ship home to
the tower. Everything in this trailer is real gameplay, recorded in the game.

• Full 6-DOF flight physics: thrust, drag, gravity, staging, heating
• The real Starbase: aerial imagery, elevation and buildings of both pads
• 9 missions, hand-flown or autopilot, plus free roam
• Catch the booster and the ship with the tower's chopsticks
• Reentry heating, belly flop, flip and landing burn
• Webcast-style telemetry, a navball cockpit and a planning map with maneuver nodes
• A full 24-hour day and night cycle

Needs a desktop or laptop computer with a keyboard, and a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
