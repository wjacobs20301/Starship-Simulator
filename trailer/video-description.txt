Starship Flight Simulator: fly SpaceX's Starship and Super Heavy in your browser.
Launch from Starbase, hand-fly the ascent or let the autopilot fly, hot staging, catch the booster
with the chopsticks, reach orbit, survive reentry and bring the ship home to the tower.
Play (on a computer): https://starship.wjacobs.space
